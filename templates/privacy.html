Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account, such as your name and email address, along with the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to sign you in, store your expenses, and show your spending summaries and filters. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on the servers that run Spendly. We take reasonable measures to protect it, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may log basic request information such as your IP address. We do not share your expense data with any other third party.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact the Spendly team through the contact details provided with the app. These will be added before launch.
